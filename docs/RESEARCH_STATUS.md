# Research status — evidence snapshot 2026-10-23

| Objective | Evidence level | Missing proof |
|---|---|---|
| **A: quality per executed FLOP** | **Not supported at ~17M**: best AM approaches MoE NLL only with more FLOP or parameters; at 0.85× routed FLOP it loses to an equal-parameter MoE by 0.044–0.054 nat (W2); original E07 gate failed | Compute-matched Pareto improvement on independent data |
| **B: inference GPU memory** | Not shown; training VRAM is not working-set savings | Actual paging/offload, allocation and PCIe metrics |
| **C: decode speed** | Not shown; exploratory W3 measurement (one RTX 3090, batch 1): after execution fixes FDC1 2,179 vs MoE 2,293 decoded tok/s, quality unchanged; at batch 32 the MoE stays faster | Preregistered decode measurement, several seeds, a second GPU type |
| **D: larger-model capabilities** | Long-term ambition, not proven | Scale and coding-task evaluations |

Supported observations: early E09 learned routing was 19.9% better in relative validation loss than **random routing**, not MoE. Per-hop unshared attention improved small-model loss while increasing capacity. The 36 overnight runs (12 configs × 3 seeds) showed H05_h8 at NLL 2.1212, MoE 2.1166, with 1.24× FLOP and +6.9% parameters. The CI includes zero; **absence of significant difference is not equivalence**. The current soft-survival/hard-truncation mismatch causes severe quality loss at shorter depth. H04 at 0.85× FLOP remains **inconclusive**.

**Round V3, W1 (preregistered, 48 runs, closed 2026-10-23):** of the four factors that separate FixedDepth from MoE, expert tying across hops (separate banks −0.020 to −0.036 nat) and hierarchical routing (flat 24-way router −0.033 nat at equal parameters and equal *routed* FLOP) each cost quality; for shared bank normalisation and the router's training rule no effect was detected (inconclusive, not equivalence). Learned routing beats random routing by 0.129 nat. The best FixedDepth variants reach the dense control, not MoE. An anytime (multi-exit) loss improves the hard halting path by 0.70–0.88 nat but stays at 1.18–1.27× the MoE loss with 0.87× routed and 1.10–1.12× executed FLOP — the preregistered A screening gate is not met. [W1 report](../results/round_v3_w1/W1_REPORT.md).

**Round V3, W2 (preregistered, 66 runs, closed 2026-10-24):** separate per-hop attention matters (shared +0.174, with adapter +0.132, every second hop expert-only +0.114 nat). At equal routed FLOP and parameters 8 hops and top-2/top-1 are not distinguishable from the base (inconclusive); 4 hops is worse. **The A-relevant matched pairs fail:** at 0.85× routed FLOP the AM-LLM is 0.044–0.054 nat worse than an equal-parameter MoE. The W1 combination *FDC1* (3 banks + flat routing) reaches 2.087 vs MoE 2.118 at equal routed FLOP, but with 1.83× parameters and 1.38× executed FLOP; its advantage shrinks during training (−0.127 at 2k steps, −0.031 at 9k), and the parameter-matched MoE control has not run yet. On the halting lane, an anytime weight of 0.5 and adaptive K (better than fixed K = 1 by 0.068 nat) still leave the hard path at ≥ 1.19× MoE. Note: the training split has 53M tokens, so every run sees it ~2.8×. [W2 report](../results/round_v3_w2/W2_REPORT.md).

**Round V3, W3 (preregistered, 15 runs + measurement of 21 checkpoints, closed 2026-10-24):** at equal parameters (31.4M) and routed FLOP the plain MoE (MoE12, 2.068) beats FDC1 (2.087) by 0.019 nat at 9k steps — FDC1's W2 lead came from its parameters. At 18k steps the two are not distinguishable (+0.001, inconclusive; 18k steps repeat the data ~5.6×). The 2×2 single factors reproduce W1: flat routing beats hierarchical routing at equal parameters (by 0.034–0.046 nat), separate banks help. The A success gate, measurable for the first time, fails for both halting models: they execute 0.80–0.86× MoE's padding-free FLOP but reach only 1.18–1.23× MoE loss. Exploratory decode benchmark: most of the AM–MoE batch-1 speed gap was execution (a per-call weight conversion, sort/scatter dispatch); with fixes AM-LLM reaches ~95% of MoE speed at batch 1, quality unchanged. [W3 report](../results/round_v3_w3/W3_REPORT.md).

**Round V3, W4 (descriptive check on an independent holdout, closed 2026-10-24):** no AM candidate passed its preregistered gate, so there is no confirmatory holdout candidate; instead the holdout part H1 (154 fresh npm packages, ~3M tokens, independent of the training corpus) was opened once to check whether V3's findings transfer. They do for flat vs hierarchical routing, the anytime loss, and FDC1 being worse than the equal-size MoE; the bank gain and FDC1's validation lead over the small MoE shrink or disappear; the 18k-step models generalize worse than their 9k versions. The second holdout part (H2) stays sealed. [W4 report](../results/round_v3_w4/W4_REPORT.md).

Limits: n=3 seeds; byte-level NLL is not directly comparable to BPE nat/token; estimated FLOP is not actual GPU kernel work or generated tokens/s; the historic planner saw test losses, contaminating that holdout. Public data lack full corpora, weights and per-run JSON, so this is an audited **secondary report**, not independent reproduction.
